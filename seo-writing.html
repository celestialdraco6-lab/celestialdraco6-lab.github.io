Featured Sample: On-Page SEO Brief + Article

Written for a graded hiring assessment (AppLaunchpad) - passed the screening round on this submission

SEO Brief

Primary Keyword 1app store screenshots 33 uses
Primary Keyword 2AppLaunchpad 47 uses
Secondary KeywordiPhone screenshot template 21 uses
Secondary Keywordapp store screenshot generator 15 uses
Word Count~2,480 words
FormatStep-by-step guide + FAQ schema

Title: "How to Generate App Store Screenshots Using a Template in AppLaunchpad"

Structured as a 9-step guide with keyword-rich H2s, benefit-led intro (hook: "Most users don't read your app description... they scroll through screenshots and decide in under 5 seconds"), a Key Takeaways block for scannability, a pricing comparison table, and a 6-question FAQ section written to target long-tail and "People Also Ask" style queries. Every image includes descriptive, keyword-aware alt text.

This sample is a full demonstration of on-page SEO from brief to finished article - keyword placement, search-intent structuring, and readability, done together rather than as separate steps. Written to a detailed brief with strict formatting, SEO, and image requirements, it was the deciding submission in a hiring screening I passed.
