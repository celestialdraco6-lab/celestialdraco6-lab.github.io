Independent Technical SEO & AEO Audit: Conscious Chemist

Self-directed audit · consciouschemist.com (Shark Tank India–featured D2C skincare brand)

A self-initiated audit, not client work - chosen deliberately to demonstrate audit methodology on a real, complex site rather than a spotless one. I scoped the review, picked the tools, ran the analysis, and wrote the recommendations myself.

Technical: Mobile Core Web Vitals fail site-wide and worsen on promo-heavy pages - a checked collection page scored LCP 3.9s, INP 518ms, and CLS 0.27, all in the failing range, likely driven by late-loading countdown timers and offer banners. Alt-text coverage is inconsistent at scale (92 of 281 homepage images missing alt text; 67 of 624 on a collection page), and sale messaging is baked into non-crawlable images rather than text.

AEO: I ran four real queries - a category search, a sensitive-skin question, a head-to-head brand comparison, and a reviews query - through ChatGPT and Perplexity. Neither engine cited consciouschemist.com directly in any of the four. Both pulled from Reddit, Nykaa, 1mg, and Flipkart instead, including one Reddit thread that visibly shaped part of ChatGPT's verdict on the reviews query.

  • Full on-page crawl: title/meta length, schema, alt-text and internal-link audits
  • Google PageSpeed Insights on mobile and desktop, homepage and collection page
  • Domain authority check via Ahrefs toolbar; manual robots.txt / sitemap.xml review
  • Four live query tests across ChatGPT and Perplexity to assess AI-answer visibility

Findings and recommendations only - this was an audit, not an implementation. No fixes were deployed and no before/after results are claimed.
