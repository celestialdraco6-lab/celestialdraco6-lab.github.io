Managed on-page SEO and content publishing for d2cbot.com's blog. Over a three-month window, total organic clicks grew 76% (Google Search Console). One blog post, optimized for a long-tail keyword around Instagram content tools, saw a 533% increase in clicks after on-page optimization. A newly published piece on AI-powered content generation for Shopify stores went from 0 to 33 clicks following publication and internal linking.

  • Keyword research and on-page optimization across the blog
  • Meta tag structuring and internal linking strategy
  • Content publishing cadence of 7 posts per week baseline

Site-wide average search position sits at 10.2 (page 2, approaching page 1) — reported honestly here as an in-progress metric, not a completed result.

Content Ops + AEO

High-Volume SEO Production & Answer Engine Optimization for Freecultr

SEO Specialist, AST Consulting · Freecultr.com (D2C innerwear & apparel)

13+

Ran a high-volume, keyword-first content operation for Freecultr — over 150 SEO-optimized blog posts published across 8 weeks, each built around a specific commercial keyword (e.g. "best bamboo fabric underwear," "seamless panties for women," "top 10 underwear brands in India"), using consistent buying-guide, comparison, and explainer formats.

Alongside this, I lead Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) for the brand — tracking how Freecultr surfaces in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity) for product-related queries, then creating and optimizing content to improve that visibility. Through weekly tracking, Freecultr holds Page 1 rankings for 13+ core keywords and appears in ChatGPT answers or source citations for 20+ tracked product-intent queries.

  • Long-tail keyword research and daily content targeting
  • 25+ product-related keywords tracked weekly for AI-search visibility
  • Content structure and messaging optimized specifically for AI citation
